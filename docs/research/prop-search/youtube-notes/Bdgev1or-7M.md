Here is the breakdown of the 5-minute scalping strategy presented in the video:

---

### 1. Instrument(s) and Chart Timeframe(s)
* **Timeframe:** 5-minute (5M) chart.
* **Instruments Shown:** EUR/USD, USD/CAD, EUR/CHF (applicable across major Forex pairs).

---

### 2. Session / Time Window
* **Asia Session:** Accumulation phase (establishes the Asian High and Low range).
* **London Session:** Manipulation phase (aggressive push sweeping Asian liquidity).
* **New York Session:** Distribution / Reversal phase (where entries are taken).
* **Open Range Time Window (Model 3):** 9:30 AM – 9:45 AM (first three 5-minute candles of the NY session). *Time zone not explicitly stated, implied EST/EDT.*

---

### 3. Setup Conditions (in Order)
1. **Accumulation (Asia):** Market must consolidate into a tight Asian Session range.
2. **Manipulation (London):** London session must exhibit an aggressive push in one direction that sweeps either the Asian Session High or Low liquidity.
3. **Distribution Setup (New York):** Prepare for a reversal during the New York Session in the opposite direction of the London push (e.g., if London pushed down and swept Asia low, look for Buys in NY).
4. **Validation Check:** 
   * If the Asian range is too wide $\rightarrow$ Accumulation failed.
   * If London lacks an aggressive push/sweep $\rightarrow$ Manipulation failed.
   * If failed, skip trading that pair for the day.

---

### 4. Entry Trigger and Order Type
Trades are entered during the NY reversal using one of three entry models:

* **Entry Model 1 (Support & Resistance):**
  * Identify a key Support level (for buys) after the London liquidity sweep.
  * **Trigger:** Price retests the support zone and prints a **Bullish Engulfing Candle Pattern** (green candle body engulfs previous red candle body).

* **Entry Model 2 (Supply & Demand):**
  * Identify a Demand Zone (the last down candle before an aggressive multi-candle push creating a Fair Value Gap / Break of Structure).
  * **Trigger:** Price returns to the Demand Zone, respects the level without closing below it, and forms a **Bullish Engulfing Candle Pattern**.

* **Entry Model 3 (Open Range Breakout - ORB):**
  * Mark the Range High and Range Low of the 9:30 AM – 9:45 AM (3 $\times$ 5-min) candles.
  * Wait for a 5-minute candle to break and close outside the range in the direction of the expected NY reversal.
  * Mark the resulting Fair Value Gap (FVG) or Demand Zone created near the breakout.
  * **Trigger:** Price retraces into the FVG/Demand Zone and prints a **Bullish Engulfing Candle Pattern**.

---

### 5. Stop-Loss Placement
* Placed directly below the Demand Zone, Support level, or Fair Value Gap structure (plus a small buffer).

---

### 6. Take-Profit / Exit Rules
* **Target:** Next obvious key structure level (opposing Supply/Resistance zone or prior session support/resistance level).
* **Risk-to-Reward Ratio:** Typically aims for approximately $1:2$ R:R or key level target.
* **Time Exits / Trailing / Partials:** Not stated.

---

### 7. Filters
* **Session Structure Filter:** Must have a valid Accumulation-Manipulation-Distribution (AMD) pattern across Asia, London, and NY sessions.
* **Candle Body Confirmation:** Breakout of open range must be a full candle body close (wicks do not count).
* **Indicators:** None used (pure price action / market structure).
* **News / Days of Week:** Not stated.

### 10. Discretionary Parts
