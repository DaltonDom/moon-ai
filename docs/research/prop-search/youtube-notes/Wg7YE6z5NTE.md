### 1. Instrument(s) and Chart Timeframe(s)
* **Instrument:** Nasdaq Futures (NQ / NQ1!).
* **Higher Timeframe (HTF):** 4-Hour (4H) candlestick (also references Daily chart for bias).
* **Lower Timeframe (LTF / Execution):** 1-Minute (1M).

---

### 2. Session / Time Window
* **Sessions:** Applicable across all trading sessions (New York AM, New York PM, London, Asia) around the open of each new 4-hour candlestick.
* **Time Zone:** New York Time (NY Midnight setting indicated on the HTF indicator).

---

### 3. Setup Conditions (In Order)
1. **Determine HTF Bias:** Establish directional bias (bullish or bearish) using daily/higher timeframe context.
2. **Identify Accumulation Range:** 
   * Look for price consolidating/moving sideways prior to the open of a new 4-hour candlestick (or within the first 10–20 minutes after open).
3. **Wait for Manipulation Wick:** 
   * **Bullish Bias:** Expect the bottom wick to form first (push lower).
   * **Bearish Bias:** Expect the top wick to form first (push higher).
4. **Liquidity / FVG Tap Requirement:** 
   * The manipulation move must sweep a key liquidity level (e.g., a significant low) or tap into an HTF Fair Value Gap (5M, 15M, 1H, or 4H FVG).

---

### 4. Entry Trigger and Order Type
* **Confirmation Entry (Change in State of Delivery - CSD):**
  1. On the 1-minute chart, after the manipulation wick sweeps liquidity/FVG, wait for a new 1-minute bullish FVG to form and hold/get respected.
  2. Wait for a 1-minute candle body closure above the high of the last downside leg (Change in State of Delivery).
  3. Enter Long (Market or Limit order) upon confirmation.
* *(Note: An aggressive entry at the absolute low of the wick is mentioned for advanced traders, but the primary method taught is waiting for 1M confirmation).*

---

### 5. Stop-Loss Placement
* Placed below the low of the 4-hour manipulation wick (the 4H low for long setups).

---

### 6. Take-Profit / Exit Rules
* **Target 1:** The top of the consolidation/accumulation range.
* **Extended Target:** Higher liquidity targets if the accumulation range is narrow, optimizing risk-to-reward ratio (video displays R:R ratios from 2.5:1 up to 7.5:1+).

* **Narrative Filter:** Manipulation moves *must* sweep a significant structural high/low or hit an HTF FVG. Pattern alone without a liquidity sweep/FVG tap is invalid.
