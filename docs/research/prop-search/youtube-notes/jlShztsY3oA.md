Here is the exact breakdown of the 15-Minute Opening Range Breakout (ORB) strategy presented in the video:

### 1. Instrument(s) and Chart Timeframe(s)
* **Timeframe:** 15-minute chart (`15m`).
* **Instruments Mentioned:** High-volatility assets such as **NAS 100**, **S&P 500**, and **Gold (XAUUSD)** (demonstrated on XAUUSD).

### 2. Session / Time Window (with Time Zone)
* **Session:** New York Open.
* **Range Window:** `09:30 - 09:45 EST` (the first 15-minute candle of the New York session).

### 3. Setup Conditions (in order)
1. Mark the **High** and **Low** of the initial 15-minute candle (`09:30 - 09:45 EST`) to define the range box.
2. Wait for a subsequent 15-minute candle to break and close completely outside of this range box.
3. **Proposed Fixes / Modifications by Speaker:**
   * Determine the higher timeframe trend/bias (`15m` or `1h`).
   * Look for confluence (trendlines, Fibonacci retracement levels, liquidity sweeps, or market structure breaks).
   * Watch for fakeouts outside the range and trade re-entries back inside the range.

### 4. Entry Trigger and Order Type
* **Long Entry:** Enter long when a 15-minute candle breaks and closes **above** the opening range high.
* **Short Entry:** Enter short when a 15-minute candle breaks and closes **below** the opening range low.
* **Order Type:** Market entry on candle close (implied visually).

### 5. Stop-Loss Placement
* Placed on the opposite side of the initial 15-minute opening range box.

### 6. Take-Profit / Exit Rules
* **Risk-to-Reward Ratio:** `1 : 1.5` R:R.
* **Time Exits / Trailing / Partials:** Not stated.

### 7. Filters
* **Trend Filter:** Only trade in the direction of the higher timeframe (`15m` or `1h`) trend.
* **Confluence Filter:** Draw trendlines and Fibonacci retracements (e.g., 0.5–0.618 Golden Zone) to avoid trading directly into major trendlines or counter-trend moves.

### 8. Max Trades Per Day / Risk Per Trade
* **Max Trades Per Day:** 1 trade per morning / session ("one clean breakout trade every morning").
* **Risk Per Trade:** Not stated explicitly (chart examples use ~1% risk per trade).

### 9. Claimed Results
* **Sample Size:** 8 trading days on Gold (XAUUSD).

### 10. Discretionary Parts
* Drawing manual trendlines and determining market structure.
* Identifying valid higher timeframe (`1h`) trend bias.
* Spotting "fakeouts" vs. legitimate breakout continuations.
* Applying Fibonacci tools to validate pullback/rejection levels.
