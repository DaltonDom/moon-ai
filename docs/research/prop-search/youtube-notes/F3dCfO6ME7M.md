Here is the detailed extraction of the Opening Range Breakout (ORB) strategy presented in the video:

---

### 1. Instrument(s) and Chart Timeframe(s)
* **Instruments:** Any tradable asset (US Stocks, Indices, Forex, Crypto).
* **ORB Timeframes:** 5-minute, 15-minute (preferred/most popular), or 30-minute charts.
* **Entry / Structure Timeframes:** 5-minute and 1-minute charts.
* **Higher Timeframe Context:** 1-Hour and Daily charts.

---

### 2. Session / Time Window (with Time Zone)
* **Sessions:** Applicable to London, New York, or Tokyo sessions.
* **Preferred Session:** **New York Session** (NYSE Open at **9:30 AM Eastern Time / ET**).
* **ORB Candle Window:** First candle directly following the 9:30 AM ET open (e.g., 9:30–9:45 AM ET on a 15-minute timeframe).

---

### 3. Setup Conditions (in Order)
1. **Define Range:** Wait for the 9:30 AM ET open candle to close. Draw a horizontal line at the top wick (**ORH** - Opening Range High) and bottom wick (**ORL** - Opening Range Low).
2. **Identify Breakout:** Wait for price action on lower timeframes to break outside the ORH or ORL. 
   * *Requirement:* Must be a candle **body close** outside the level. A wick beyond the level without a close is considered a false breakout/sweep.
3. **SMC / PD Array Confluence (Preferred Setup - Option 2):**
   * **Bullish Breakout:** Price closes above ORH with an impulsive move creating a 5m or 15m **Bullish Fair Value Gap (FVG)** that overlaps with the ORH level.
   * **Bearish / False Breakout Setup:** Price wicks above ORH (or breaks and rapidly fails), creating a 5m or 15m **Bearish FVG** overlapping the ORH level, signaling rejection.

---

### 4. Entry Trigger and Order Type
* **Option 1 (Breakout Entry):** Enter immediately upon the close of the first candle that closes outside the ORH/ORL.
* **Option 2 (Retest Entry - Preferred):** Wait for price to pull back and retest the ORH/ORL level or the overlapping FVG (5m/15m TF) and enter upon rejection.
* **Option 3 (2nd Breakout Entry):** Wait for price to retest, then enter when a subsequent candle breaks the swing high/low formed by the initial breakout move.

---

### 5. Stop-Loss Placement
* Place a "tight/sensible stop-loss" below the FVG / inside the ORB range.
* *Invalidation:* For a long setup, if a candle closes below the FVG / back inside the range, the trade is invalidated. 
* *(Note: Exact pip/tick offset or swing-anchor rule is not explicitly specified in numbers).*

---

### 6. Take-Profit / Exit Rules
* Target external liquidity levels such as **Previous Day High (PDH)**, **Previous Day Low (PDL)**, higher timeframe swing highs/lows, or the opposite side of the ORB range (e.g., targeting ORL on a false breakout from ORH).
* Specific reward-to-risk ratios or partial exit rules are *not stated*.

---

### 7. Filters
* **HTF Context Filter:** Always check 1H/Daily levels. Avoid taking long breakout trades directly into major liquidity pools like the Previous Day High (PDH), as these often trigger liquidity sweeps and reversals.
* **PD Array Timeframe Filter:** Do not rely on 1-minute FVGs alone due to noise; use at least 5-minute or 15-minute FVGs for entry confluence.
