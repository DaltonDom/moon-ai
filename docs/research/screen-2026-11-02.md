# Screen 1: simple intraday rules (2026-11-02)

`scripts/research/screen.py` checks variants quickly on research data only (2019-06 up to 2025-11-01), MNQ and MES. It follows the engine's fill and cost rules.

**Calibration:** the 5-minute ORB gives 1,553 trades and +149.8R here, against 1,522 trades and +124.4R in the engine. The screen is about 20% optimistic, mainly because it doesn't apply news blackouts or skip holidays. That is acceptable for a filter, since survivors are re-run in the engine.

**What was screened:** 172 variants across six templates. Full results are in `screen-2026-11-02.csv`.
- time-of-day momentum
- gap and go
- previous-day high/low breakout
- overnight-range breakout with wider stops
- range expansion after a quiet first 30 or 60 minutes
- previous-day trend, followed or faded

The luck test counts 274 versions (these 172 plus the 102 engine-tested before).

**Result: no survivor.** Survival needed MNQ DSR above 0.95 and a positive MES mean.

**The pattern:** all 15 best variants are the same idea. At 10:30 (60 minutes in), trade the direction of the first hour's move with a tight stop (0.25 ATR), usually with a 2R target.

| Variant | MNQ trades | MNQ mean | MNQ Sharpe/yr | DSR | MES mean | MES Sharpe/yr |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| m=60, move > 0.5 ATR, k=0.25, target 2R | 189 | +0.416R | 1.56 | 0.73 | +0.187R | 0.63 |
| m=60, any move, k=0.25, target 2R | 1,555 | +0.112R | 1.39 | 0.55 | +0.002R | 0.02 |
| m=120, move > 0.5 ATR, k=0.25, target 2R | 339 | +0.229R | 1.32 | 0.48 | +0.052R | 0.26 |
| m=60, move > 0.25 ATR, k=0.25, target 2R | 687 | +0.163R | 1.29 | 0.44 | +0.067R | 0.47 |

**Reading:**
- First-hour momentum is the most consistent effect found so far. It is the same morning momentum that kept showing up in batches 1 to 4.
- It is stronger on MNQ than on MES.
- Six and a half years can't separate an edge of this size (Sharpe about 1.3 to 1.6) from luck once 274 versions have been tried.

**What would settle it:** data nobody has looked at. NQ and ES history from 2010 to 2019 has never been used in this project. Pre-register a few variants now, then test them once on 2010–2019. That is a second, independent check, before the 2025–26 holdout and paper trading.
