## 1. Bottom line

- **Build a NinjaScript AddOn (not a Strategy, not the ATI) that runs a small TCP server and places orders through `Account.CreateOrder()` / `Account.Submit()`.** The AddOn, not Python, owns the bracket: when the entry fills, it submits the stop and target itself, with a fresh OCO ID. Python going away then never leaves a position without a stop.
- **The ATI (order instruction files or the DLL) works but is the weaker choice.** It is file-polling or a DLL that NinjaTrader staff have said was broken on some 8.1 builds, and "external programming languages" are "not documented or supported".
- **Where the stop lives depends on the firm's connection, not on our code.** NinjaTrader's own guide says CQG/Continuum holds OCO natively on its servers, while for **Rithmic, OCO is "simulated on your local PC"**. Working stops survive NT dying on both. On Rithmic (Bulenox), the *pairing* of stop and target does not, so a target can be left working after the stop fills.
- **Data:** NT8's free trial gives **14 days** of real-time data in the simulator. After that it is delayed, or **Sim+ at $35/mo** (US, no live NinjaTrader Brokerage account) keeps Level 1 CME live. All three firms include non-professional CME data in the evaluation fee.
- **Firms:** all three allow automation in their own words. Lucid and MFFU evaluations sign in to NT8 with **Tradovate** credentials, with Lucid labelling it the "CQG data feed". Bulenox is **Rithmic** and requires management approval of third-party automation, plus possibly a $100/mo API fee.
- **A test on Sim101 cannot prove broker-side bracket survival**, because Sim101 orders live inside NinjaTrader. The crash drills in §5 must be repeated once at minimum size on the evaluation account.

## 2. Bridge options

Shared background, from NinjaTrader's own help guide:

- **Where working orders live.** NinjaTrader's guide says Accepted or Working orders are held by the broker or exchange. "For Continuum/CQG, most OCO functionality is natively supported on their servers." For Rithmic, OCO is "simulated on your local PC" ([Where do your orders reside?](https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/where_do_your_orders_reside_.htm), read 2026-10-31).
- **Behaviour when NT closes.** NinjaTrader staff say: "Any disconnection of NT will not affect orders which are already submitted to the exchange" ([forum: Are stops stored server side?](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/platform-technical-support-aa/94292-are-stops-stored-server-side)). Where OCO is not native, "NinjaTrader needs to be running and connected to send the cancellation request of the remaining OCO order", and with Rithmic, "both exit orders can execute" if NT is shut down ([forum: Open Order Handling when computer is disconnected](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/platform-technical-support-aa/1134269-open-order-handling-when-computer-is-disconnected)).
- **The naked window.** Every NT8 route submits the stop and target *after* the entry fills, because NT8 exposes no broker-native "order-sends-order" bracket. If the entry fills while NT is down, nothing submits the stop ([forum: same thread](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/platform-technical-support-aa/1134269-open-order-handling-when-computer-is-disconnected); NinjaTrader Brokerage "does not support order places order orders" per staff, [OPO at CQG thread](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-7/strategy-development-aa/1178241-opo-order-places-order-at-cqg)). The window is the time from fill to stop acceptance. It is small, but it is the main residual risk, and §5 measures it.
- **Simulated stops are local.** "SS orders are held and simulated locally on your PC and are therefore subject to issues such as loss of internet connection and computer crashes." They are not the default ([Simulated Stop Orders](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/simulated_stop_orders.htm)). The bridge must never use them.
- **Server-side ATMs (beta)** keep ATM logic on NinjaTrader's servers, but the help guide says they are exclusive to the NinjaTrader connection and need Multi-provider off. Staff say "server side atms are … not available in NinjaScript" ([Server Side vs Local ATMs](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/server-side-vs-local-atms.htm); [forum 1298238](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/strategy-development/1298238-a-server-side-atm-via-a-strategy-or-indicator), April 2024). They are not usable from our bridge. Whether that has changed by 8.1.8 is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Maintenance.** NT8 is actively released: 8.1.8.0 shipped on 2026-07-21, and 8.1.8.3 (2026-09-29) fixed "prop-provider accounts moving to the new connectivity", with a further prop-connectivity fix on 2026-10-19 ([Release Notes 8.1.8](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/8_1_8.htm)). Prop connectivity is still changing, so the bridge should pin an NT version during the evaluation.

### 2.1 Option A: NinjaScript AddOn with a TCP socket (recommended)

- **What it is.** A C# AddOn loaded by NT8 at start-up. It opens a TCP listener for Python and calls the AddOn Account API. Orders are built with `Account.CreateOrder()`, which takes an `oco` string, and sent with `Account.Submit()`. Fills and order states arrive through the `Account.OrderUpdate` and `ExecutionUpdate` events ([CreateOrder()](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/createorder.htm), [Submit()](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/submit.htm), [OrderUpdate](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/orderupdate.htm), [Account class](https://ninjatrader-live.ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/account_class.htm)).
- **Bracket.** Python sends one message: `ENTER side qty entry_type stop_px target_px client_id`. The AddOn submits the entry. In the `ExecutionUpdate` handler for that entry, it immediately submits a stop-market and a limit for the filled quantity, sharing a **new** OCO ID. It resizes them on partial fills. NinjaTrader's own reference sample calls submitting protective orders from the execution callback "the fastest possible submission of protective orders" ([Using OnOrderUpdate() and OnExecution() to submit protective orders](https://ninjatrader.com/pt/support/helpGuides/nt8/using_onorderupdate_and_onexec.htm)). OCO rules to respect: "Once any order of this group is either filled or cancelled, all other orders that belong to this group will be cancelled". A spent OCO ID must not be reused, and from 8.1.3.0 OCO IDs reset after fill, cancel or reject ([CreateOrder()](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/createorder.htm); [forum 101942](https://ninjatrader.com/support/forum/forum/ninjatrader-8/platform-technical-support-aa/101942-error-with-oco-id-in-automated-strategy)).
- **Why an AddOn and not a Strategy.** A NinjaScript *Strategy*'s orders are tied to its lifetime. On restart, "Wait Until Flat" cancels its orders, and "Immediately Submit" cancels any live order that doesn't match its historical replay ([forum 1103486](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/strategy-development/1103486-need-an-advise-on-which-syncing-account-positions-method-to-use)). A 2025–26 community report on a Tradovate connection says "all bracket orders on existing positions get cancelled" when NT8 restarts, and that `SetStopLoss`/`SetProfitTarget` don't attach to adopted positions. The suggested fix is to submit through `Account.Submit` so the orders are independent of the strategy ([discourse 6777](https://discourse.ninjatrader.com/t/bracket-orders-cancelled-after-nt8-restart-any-solution-for-managed-strategies/6777)). That report is from a community user, not NinjaTrader staff. Account-level orders from an AddOn avoid this whole class of problem.
- **Python crash.** Nothing happens to the position, because the stop and target are already working at the broker. The AddOn should run a **heartbeat watchdog**. If no heartbeat arrives for N seconds, it cancels unfilled *entry* orders and never touches exits. Optionally, it flattens if the design wants "no engine means no position".
- **NT crash or PC loss.** Working stops and targets stay at the broker (see above). On a CQG-native-OCO route, the pair stays linked. On Rithmic (Bulenox), the surviving leg stays working, and the AddOn's start-up reconciliation must cancel it. Two mitigations on Rithmic: rest only the **stop** at the broker and hold the target in the AddOn, or accept the orphan and clean it up on reconnect. An entry that fills while NT is down is naked. Use **market or marketable-limit entries only**, never resting entry orders, so that window does not exist.
- **Reconnect.** On start-up and on every `ConnectionStatusUpdate` back to Connected, the AddOn reconciles. For each account position on the instrument, it checks for exactly one working stop of the right size and side. A missing stop is submitted at once at the journal's stop price, or the position is flattened if the price is unknown or already crossed. Orphan exits with no position are cancelled. The full state is reported to Python. NT's own strategy connection-loss settings (Keep Running / Recalculate / Stop) don't apply to an AddOn, so the AddOn writes its own ([ConnectionLossHandling](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/connectionlosshandling.htm)).
- **Threading.** Keep socket I/O on its own thread, never block NT's dispatcher, and use `Dispatcher.InvokeAsync` only for UI work. NT warns that synchronous `Dispatcher.Invoke()` "can potentially result in deadlock scenarios" ([Multi-Threading Consideration](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/multi-threading.htm)). One community report links an AddOn holding a Socket.IO connection to broker disconnects, but the cause is unresolved ([discourse 6605](https://discourse.ninjatrader.com/t/broker-connection-drops-while-custom-addon-holds-socket-io-connection-possible-causes/6605)). Use a plain TCP socket, not Socket.IO.
- **Latency.** No first-party figure was found. On localhost, a TCP round trip is sub-millisecond, and the broker round trip dominates (**UNVERIFIED** estimate). For a 1-minute-bar strategy this is irrelevant. Measure it in §5.
- **Support.** NinjaTrader staff say NT "is capable of executing standard C# code which includes doing network operations" ([forum 107232](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/add-on-development/107232-tcp-server-share-informations)). The AddOn Account API is documented and current. The socket layer is our own code, and NinjaTrader won't debug it.
- **Where Python runs.** The design keeps the engine on the Mac. Two choices: run the engine on the Windows PC and bind the AddOn to `127.0.0.1`, or keep it on the Mac with the AddOn listening on the LAN behind a shared-secret handshake. Co-locating removes a network hop and a failure mode. That is a design choice for the user, not a research finding.

### 2.2 Option B: ATI, order instruction files (OIF)

- **What it is.** A text file `oif*.txt` dropped into NT's `incoming` folder. One semicolon-delimited line per command: `PLACE;<ACCOUNT>;<INSTRUMENT>;<ACTION>;<QTY>;<ORDER TYPE>;[LIMIT PRICE];[STOP PRICE];<TIF>;[OCO ID];[ORDER ID];[STRATEGY];[STRATEGY ID]`. The ORDER ID and STRATEGY ID "must be unique for each line/file", and STRATEGY must name an existing ATM template ([Order Instruction Files](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/order_instruction_files_oif.htm), [Commands and Valid Parameters](https://ninjatrader-live.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/commands_and_valid_parameters.htm)). ATI must be switched on under Tools › Options › Automated trading interface ([ATI settings](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/options_ati.htm)).
- **Bracket.** Name an ATM template in the PLACE line, and NT submits the template's stop and target when the entry fills. That is a **local** (client-side) ATM, so it carries the same naked-window and Rithmic-OCO caveats as Option A. A NinjaTrader forum developer notes: "Automatic actions that happen in the platform when using NinjaScript … are not going to happen with the AT interface … you would need to be specific … and close the position to terminate the associated ATM" ([forum 1312603](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/strategy-development/1312603-auto-close-atms-for-at-orders)). ATM stop and target distances are fixed per template, so a per-trade structural stop needs either one template per distance or a later CHANGE command.
- **Crash and reconnect.** The orders are the same as Option A once they're working. Feedback is weak: order and position state come back only as files in an `outgoing` folder (**UNVERIFIED**; the folder name is from memory, not from an excerpt). One forum user had a CHANGE file fail after a fill, with no resolution posted ([forum 1180016](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/platform-technical-support-aa/1180016-oif-order-instruction-file-questions-change-not-working?p=1180020)). There is no heartbeat, so a dead Python is indistinguishable from a quiet one.
- **Latency.** NT polls the folder for files. No first-party figure was found (**UNVERIFIED**). Still fine for 1-minute bars.
- **Fit with a Mac engine.** It needs a shared folder from Mac to Windows, which adds an SMB failure mode.

### 2.3 Option C: ATI, DLL interface (NTDirect.dll / NinjaTrader.Client.dll)

- **What it is.** Functions such as `Command`, `MarketPosition` and `Orders` talk to NT over TCP, by default `localhost:36973` ([DLL Interface](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/dll_interface.htm), [Functions](https://static.ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/functions.htm)). The `Command` function takes the same verbs as the OIF, so the bracket story is the same as Option B.
- **Maintenance and support.** NinjaTrader staff in 2022–23: the DLL "does not currently work with NinjaTrader 8.0.27.1 or 8.1.1.0", and later it "has not been fixed yet for … 8.1.1.0". They suggested the managed `NinjaTrader.Client.dll` instead, and said "using external programming languages is not documented or supported" ([forum 1227713](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/platform-technical-support-aa/1227713-problem-with-ntdirect-dll-since-updating-to-8-0-27-0-64-bit), [forum 1215836](https://forum.ninjatrader.com/forum/ninjatrader-8/platform-technical-support-aa/1215836-using-ntdirect-dll-with-nt8)). The client DLL is .NET and should target .NET 4.8 ([vendor guide](https://vendor-support.ninjatrader.com/s/article/Developer-Guide-Using-the-API-DLL-with-an-external-application?language=en_US)). From Python that means `pythonnet` on Windows only. Whether the DLL works on 8.1.8 is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Verdict.** It has the same capability as Option B, plus a platform-version risk, and it is Windows-only on the Python side.

| NinjaScript Strategy with a socket | Works, but orders are tied to the strategy lifetime (see §2.1, "Why an AddOn and not a Strategy") |

### 2.5 Recommendation

**Option A, a NinjaScript AddOn with a localhost (or authenticated-LAN) TCP server, placing account-level orders.** The AddOn submits the stop and target in the entry's execution callback and uses only market or marketable entries. It runs a Python heartbeat watchdog that cancels entries but never exits, and on every start-up or reconnect it reconciles and rebuilds any missing stop. On Rithmic (Bulenox), it either rests only the stop at the broker or cleans up orphan exits on reconnect. Option B (OIF plus an ATM template) is the fallback if writing C# proves too costly, because it needs no compiled code.

## 4. Prop-firm fit

Note for `docs/design.md`: its Stack section says "NinjaTrader connects to the firm over CQG". That is true only for Lucid's NT route, which is a Tradovate login on the CQG feed. Bulenox is Rithmic, and MFFU's NT route is a Tradovate login.

### 4.1 Lucid Trading (first choice)

- **Connection.** Lucid's setup guide has a "Step 1 – CQG Data Feed — Tradovate / NinjaTrader", and new users "must sign into Tradovate first even if they plan to trade on NinjaTrader" ([Lucid Setup Guide](https://lucidtrading.com/lucid-setup-guide/), read 2026-10-31). The CQG platforms are NinjaTrader, Tradovate and TradingView. The Rithmic platforms are Quantower, Sierra Chart, R|Trader and others ([Supported Platforms](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/11404614-lucid-trading-supported-platforms), read 2026-10-31). In NT8 this is the Tradovate evaluation login: enter the evaluation credentials at the NT8 splash screen, or add a NinjaTrader connection with account type **Simulation** ([NT vendor guide: Tradovate Evaluation Service Connection](https://vendor-support.ninjatrader.com/s/article/Connecting-To-Evaluation-Services-via-Tradovate-Connection?language=en_US)).
- **Automation.** "Automated trading systems and trade copiers are permitted. All automated activity must comply with Lucid Trading rules. Traders are fully responsible for any software errors, malfunctions, or unintended outcomes." ([Other Activities](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/11404728-other-activities), read 2026-10-31). The FAQ says: "Automated strategies and in-platform/third party trade copiers are permitted, but traders are responsible for any software errors or unintended outcomes." ([FAQ](https://lucidtrading.com/general-faq/), read 2026-10-31). HFT and microscalping are banned and detected automatically ([HFT](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/11404736-prohibited-high-frequency-trading), [Microscalping](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/11404742-prohibited-microscalping)).
- **Flag for the user.** The LucidDirect Account Agreement (2025-05-19) requires "written approval from us prior to using any Automated Trading Software" ([PDF](https://lucidtrading.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/LucidDirect-Account-Agreement-5-19.pdf); quote from the companion doc). Under the rules policy this is an explicit step, so the user decides. It is LucidDirect-specific, and whether it applies to LucidFlex is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **NT8 + automation works?** By the rules, yes. Technically, Tradovate-backed evaluations connect to NT8 natively. Whether a NinjaScript AddOn may submit orders on a Tradovate *evaluation* connection with a free NT licence is **UNVERIFIED**; see §6.

### 4.2 Bulenox (second choice)

- **Connection.** Rithmic. "NinjaTrader 8 comes with a free licence on the Master Account" ([FAQ](https://bulenox.com/faq), read 2026-10-31). Setup is: enter the licence key under Help, restart, and add "Rithmic for NinjaTrader Brokerage" with the Bulenox User ID, system "Rithmic" ([Bulenox help, RU](https://bulenox.com/ru/help/), read 2026-10-31). Whether NT8 can trade a **Qualification** (evaluation) account without a paid NT licence is **UNVERIFIED**. - **Automation.** From the FAQ: bots, algorithms and trade copiers are permitted only if user-built for the account owner's own use. "Commercial, shared, rented or publicly distributed automation tools are not allowed." ([FAQ](https://bulenox.com/faq), read 2026-10-31). The Terms at sign-up say third-party algorithms and automated software "must be reviewed and approved by the Bulenox management team before use". They prohibit "scalping" algorithms, the "DTC Protocol Bridge API" and "automated discretional trading". A $100/mo third-party API or Trader Composite fee "does not constitute approval of any specific algorithm" ([Account SignUp terms](https://bulenox.com/member/signup), read 2026-10-31; older [Terms PDF, July 2021](https://bulenox.com/legal/Terms_of_Use.pdf)).
- **Flags for the user.** (1) Management approval is explicitly required, so flag it. (2) Whether NT8 driven by our own AddOn counts as a "third-party API or composite connection" owing $100/mo is **UNVERIFIED**. NT8 itself is the Rithmic client here, and Python never touches Rithmic. (3) Rithmic OCO is local to the PC, so the §2.1 orphan-exit handling is mandatory.

## 5. Connection-test pass/fail criteria

These are proposed for the 14-day Phase 3 test on Sim101 with trial real-time data. Every metric comes from the bridge's own log joined to the engine journal. The test fails if **any** hard criterion fails.

**Hard criteria (all must hold over all 14 sessions):**

1. **Bracketing.** 100% of filled entries have a working stop accepted within **≤ 1 s** of the entry fill timestamp, and a target within ≤ 1 s, unless the Rithmic stop-only mode is chosen. The p99 fill-to-stop-accepted time is reported.
2. **No naked exposure.** At every 1-second sample, account position ≠ 0 implies exactly one working stop on the correct side, for the full position quantity.
3. **Signal integrity.** 0 missed signals and 0 duplicate orders: every engine signal maps to exactly one entry (by `client_id`) or to a logged, rule-based rejection (risk rule, news blackout, max trades).
4. **Order acknowledgement.** 100% of commands sent by Python receive an ack or reject from the AddOn within ≤ 2 s. Median and p99 round-trip are recorded.
5. **Fill fidelity.** Each fill is compared with the Phase 2 replay of the same session (Databento 1-minute, plus 1-second bars where available): market-entry slippage **≤ 2 ticks** median and **≤ 4 ticks** p95. Stop and target fills land exactly at the order price ±1 tick (sim fills; real slippage is measured later on the evaluation).
6. **Hard risk rules enforced by the engine and confirmed by the AddOn log:** ≤ 2 trades per session; flat by 16:00 ET every day, with the last position closed by **15:59:30 ET**; no entry inside a news blackout; daily stop at −2% halts new entries for the rest of the session. At least one daily-stop trip must be forced on a test day, for example with a temporary tiny limit, to prove it.
7. **Crash drills, each run at least twice, while in a position:**
   - (a) **Kill Python.** The position stays bracketed, the AddOn cancels any unfilled entry within the heartbeat timeout (proposed **5 s**), and when Python restarts it reconciles in ≤ 10 s.
   - (b) **Kill NinjaTrader.** On restart, the AddOn reconciles in ≤ 30 s after reconnect: the stop exists, orphans are cancelled, and state matches the journal.
   - (c) **Pull the network for 60 s.** NT reconnects automatically. The AddOn reconciles in **≤ 30 s** of reconnect, and the safety alert fires if the outage is longer than 30 s with a position open.
   - (d) **Kill switch.** It flattens and cancels everything in ≤ 5 s.
8. **Restart hygiene.** Any morning start of NT plus the engine with a flat account produces zero stray orders.

**Soft criteria (report, investigate if missed):**

- Uptime of the AddOn connection during RTH ≥ 99.5%. Every disconnect is logged with its duration.
- Bar parity: the engine's 1-minute OHLC from NT data matches Databento for the same minute within 1 tick on ≥ 99% of bars. Bar-time alignment is checked at session open.

**Caveat.** Sim101 orders live inside NinjaTrader, so drills (b) and (c) on Sim101 prove our reconciliation code but *not* broker-side survival or broker OCO. Run drills (a)–(c) once more on the evaluation account, at 1 MNQ, in the first days, before the strategy trades normally.
